Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with the predictor?

Sign up for an account, login, and head to the Premium Predictor tool. Answer a few questions about your health and lifestyle, and we'll calculate your personalized insurance premium.

Can I change my information after prediction?

Yes! You can update your health information, dependents, or lifestyle choices at any time. Your premium will be recalculated automatically.

How accurate is the premium calculation?

Our AI-powered calculations are based on 2024 actuarial standards and historical health data. They provide a reliable estimate but may vary based on individual circumstances.

What about data privacy?

Your data is protected with industry-standard encryption and security. Read our privacy policy for detailed information about data handling.

How do I file a claim?

Visit our Claims page to learn the step-by-step process. You can also contact our support team for personalized assistance.
