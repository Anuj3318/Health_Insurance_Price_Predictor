Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2024

1. Data Collection

We collect personal information to provide you with the best insurance advisory experience. This includes:

  • Name, email address, and contact information
  • Health and medical history for premium calculation
  • Financial information for policy recommendations
  • Usage data and preferences

2. Data Security

Your data is protected with industry-standard encryption and security measures. We use SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission and maintain secure servers with regular security audits.

3. Data Usage

We use your information only for:

  • Providing personalized insurance recommendations
  • Processing claims and policy management
  • Improving our services
  • Communicating important updates
  • Compliance with legal regulations

4. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request corrections or updates
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Data portability

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with trusted partners when necessary for service delivery, always with proper data protection agreements.
